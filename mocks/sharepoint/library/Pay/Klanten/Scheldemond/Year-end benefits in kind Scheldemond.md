This note supplements the *Year-end bonus and tax forms procedure (BE)* for Scheldemond Logistics. It replaces the 2023 checklist, which predates the flat-rate agreement for phone subscriptions and must no longer be used.

## 1. Benefits in kind at Scheldemond

| Benefit | Employees (approx.) | How it is valued |
|---|---|---|
| Company car | 85 | Standard formula based on CO₂ and catalogue value, monthly |
| Phone subscription | 240 | **Flat rate agreed with the client**, not the actual cost |
| Laptop for private use | 180 | Standard flat rate |
| Home internet | 60 | Standard flat rate |

## 2. On the annual tax form

1. Every benefit in kind appears on a **separate line** (code 250).
2. The same amounts are already part of the taxable gross. **Do not add them twice.**
3. The phone subscription is reported at the agreed flat rate, even if the invoice from the operator is higher.

## 3. Checks before the run

- Compare the number of company cars with the fleet list the client sends in November.
- Employees who joined or left during the year: benefits pro rata per full month.
- Employees who switched from a company car to a mobility budget: check the switch date with the client.

## 4. Known pitfalls

- In 2024 the phone benefit was reported at actual cost for 30 employees, which led to corrections in March. The flat rate is the rule.
- The client sometimes sends the fleet list as a PDF. Ask for the Excel version.

## Unrelated: parking

From January the client's Antwerp site has a new visitor parking policy. Consultants visiting the client should register their licence plate at reception.
