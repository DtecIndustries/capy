Configuration note for registering sickness of part-time employees at Scheldemond Logistics in Time.

> **Correction in version 2.0.** Version 1.0 registered the first day of sickness of part-timers as an unpaid waiting day (AB-01). That was based on the superseded version 1.0 of the Pay sick leave procedure. The Pay team lead ruled that the guaranteed salary starts on day one, also for part-timers. Day one is now AB-10.

## 1. Background

The client asked how the first day of sickness of part-time employees should be registered. Ruling by the Pay team lead (K. Janssens), with reference to the sick leave procedure version 2.0 and the Scheldemond sick leave addendum.

## 2. Configuration

| Day of sickness | Code | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 onwards | AB-10 | Sickness, guaranteed salary |

Full-time and part-time employees are treated the same. AB-01 is not used for sickness at Scheldemond.

## 3. Implementation

- Rule in the Scheldemond absence profile *PT-SICK* changed: the AB-01 step for day 1 is removed.
- Absences registered with AB-01 since 1 September are corrected to AB-10 before the September close.
- Applies to all part-time contracts at the three sites (Antwerp, Ghent, Zeebrugge).

## 4. Test cases

| Case | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Part-timer sick Monday–Wednesday | Mon–Wed AB-10 |
| Part-timer sick 16 days | All days AB-10 |
| Full-timer sick 2 days | Both days AB-10 |
